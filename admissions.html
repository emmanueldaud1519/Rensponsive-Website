Admissions

Join a school that develops confident learners.

Admission requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Birth certificate or national identity document
  • Academic transcript or recent school report
  • Recent passport-size photographs

How to apply

Parents and guardians may visit the school office, submit the required documents, and arrange a meeting with the admissions team.

Prospective students are also welcome to attend open days and school tours before admission.
