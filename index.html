About our school

Building knowledge, values, and confidence.

Iyunga Secondary School is a forward-looking institution committed to nurturing academically strong, socially responsible, and spiritually grounded young people. We believe every learner deserves a safe, encouraging, and inspiring environment to thrive.

Our school culture balances academic excellence with creativity, leadership, and service. Through well-trained teachers, practical learning, and a strong pastoral system, we help students become resilient and ready for the future.

Our Vision

To become a leading center of excellence in secondary education in Tanzania.

Our Mission

To empower students through quality teaching, mentorship, and character formation.

Founded 2008
Location Iyunga, Tanzania
