About the school

Knowledge, values, and opportunity.

Iyunga Secondary School is a vibrant learning community committed to empowering young people to become confident, disciplined, and capable citizens. We provide a balanced educational experience that combines high academic standards with character formation and leadership development.

Our school promotes respect, integrity, creativity, and accountability. Through well-supported teachers, active learning methods, and a safe campus environment, students grow into independent thinkers ready for further education and community leadership.

  • Strong academic focus and student support
  • Safe, inclusive, and respectful learning environment
  • Active participation in sports, arts, and leadership
  • Commitment to service and responsibility in the community

Our values

What guides our school community.

Academic excellence

We encourage focus, motivation, and achievement through structured learning and academic mentorship.

Discipline and integrity

Students are encouraged to act responsibly, respectfully, and honestly in all areas of school life.

Leadership and service

We create opportunities for leadership, teamwork, and meaningful contribution to society.

Innovation and creativity

We support curiosity and imagination through projects, arts, technology, and co-curricular activities.
